How it worksGitHub

Your checkout, shopped by an honest customer.

FairCheckout sends Kane, a browser agent, through your store like a real customer with one simple goal. It catches dark patterns (sneaked add-ons, hidden fees, fake timers, cancel mazes), shows exactly where it happened, names the rule it breaks, hands the fix to your coding agent, and shops again to prove the fix.

The loop

1. ShopKane runs six honest-customer goals in a real browser, like "buy one tee and nothing else".
2. CatchA goal that can't be met honestly is a dark pattern, with Kane's steps and an evidence pack.
3. FixFIXES.md gives your coding agent each finding, the fix and the rule it breaks.
4. ProveKane shops again. The audit exits non-zero until the store is clean, so it can gate CI.

Run it on your store

npm install -g @testmuai/kane-cli && kane-cli login --oauth
git clone https://github.com/Ankith-m1006/faircheckout && cd faircheckout
# set your store's URL, one product and its price in faircheckout.config.json
node bin/faircheckout.mjs audit --url https://your-store.example/

Why it matters

India
The CCPA's 2023 guidelines ban 13 dark patterns, and in June 2025 it asked e-commerce platforms to audit themselves.
EU
The Digital Services Act and consumer law forbid interfaces that distort choice, pre-ticked paid extras and hard cancellation.
US
The FTC treats hidden fees and cancel mazes as unfair or deceptive; ROSCA requires simple cancellation.
