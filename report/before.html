Dark pattern

Basket sneaking

Nothing is added to the cart that the customer did not choose

The cart added the ₹99 protection-plan amount even though the run only added the Cotton Tee and made no cart-page changes. With the ₹499 item and ₹49 shipping, the expected total is ₹548; the displayed ₹647 includes an extra ₹99.
Investigate why the protection plan is included in the cart total without a cart-page selection. The total should remain ₹548 unless the shopper explicitly opts in to the ₹99 plan.
evidence: objective_contradiction: the Total shown on the cart page equals ₹548

Fix: Leave every paid add-on (plans, insurance, donations) unticked and out of the cart until the customer chooses it.

  • India: CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023, Annexure 1 (2) Basket sneaking
  • EU: Consumer Rights Directive Art. 22 (no pre-ticked boxes for extra payments)
  • US: FTC Act Sec. 5 (unfair or deceptive practices)
